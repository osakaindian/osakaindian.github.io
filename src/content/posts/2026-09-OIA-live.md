The Osaka University Indian Association (OIA) community platform is now live.

OIA brings together Indian students, researchers, faculty, alumni, and friends of the Indian community at Osaka University. Through this platform, we aim to make it easier for our community to stay connected, share information, and learn from one another.

Alongside our events and activities, the Posts section will be used to share:

- Community announcements and important updates
- Research, publications, and academic achievements
- Scholarships, research positions, internships, and other opportunities

We also welcome members of the community to share information and experiences that may be useful to others.

Have something to share with the OIA community? Get in touch with us.
