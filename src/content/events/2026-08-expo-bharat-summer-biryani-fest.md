The Japan Expo Bharat Association is proud to announce Expo Bharat Fest 2026 AUTUMN, taking place this autumn! Join us for two days of food, shopping, and fun.

## Biryani Festival

Authentic biryani dishes from across Japan, carefully selected, are coming together in Osaka. Discover regional flavors you wouldn't normally encounter and experience exquisite dishes rich with the aroma of carefully chosen spices.

## About the organizer

The Japan Expo Bharat Association keeps the spirit of the Bharat Pavilion at the Osaka-Kansai Expo alive after the Expo. It preserves photos, videos, and materials from the pavilion, holds post-Expo events and monthly Indian cooking classes, and offers kits to recreate popular Expo dishes such as biryani, butter chicken, and pani puri. Its longer-term aim is to build a lasting hub in Japan for Indian culture and for exchange between Japan and India.
