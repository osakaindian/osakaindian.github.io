One of Osaka's best-known fireworks festivals returns this October. Around 20,000 fireworks will light up the autumn night sky above the Yodogawa River, with displays set to popular music, underwater fireworks, and more. Families, friends, and first-time visitors are all welcome.

*This event is not organized by us. We are sharing it as one of the highlights of the season in Osaka.*

## The evening

The festival takes place on **Saturday, October 17, 2026**, with the fireworks starting around **7:30 pm**. It goes ahead rain or shine and is cancelled only in severe weather. Watching from the riverbank is free, and paid seating is also available. Seat prices vary, so please check the official site.

Expect large crowds, since the festival typically draws hundreds of thousands of visitors. Arriving early to find a good spot is a good idea.

## Registration

No registration is needed to watch from the riverbank. For paid seats, please see the official website.

## Getting there

**Yodogawa Riverbed, Osaka** (淀川河川敷)  
The display is along the Yodogawa River, from the Shin-Midosuji / Shin-Yodogawa Bridge area down to the Route 2 / Yodogawa Bridge area.

Juso Station and Nishinakajima-Minamigata Station are the nearest stations. Please check the map and plan your route in advance.
