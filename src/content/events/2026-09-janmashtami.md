Celebrate Janmashtami and Ganesh Chaturthi with the Indian community in Osaka. Join Utsavmanch for an evening of devotion, culture, vibrant performances, festive activities, and togetherness. Everyone is welcome.

## The evening

The celebration takes place from **6:30 pm to 10:00 pm**, featuring cultural performances, festive activities, and celebrations of Janmashtami and Ganesh Chaturthi.

## Registration

**Registration is required for entry.**

The registration fee is **¥1,500 per person for those above 10 years of age**. Children below 10 years can attend free of charge.

A group rate of **¥5,000 is available for 4 adults** (family or friends).

Please register in advance using the registration form.
