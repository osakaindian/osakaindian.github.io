The 3rd India Fashion & Lifestyle Show Osaka 2026 was held for three days, from Wednesday, June 17 to Friday, June 19, 2026, at MyDome Osaka (3F Hall), and concluded successfully. We would like to express our sincere gratitude to all visitors as well as to the organizations that supported and cooperated with the show.

## The show

Held under the special patronage of the Ministry of Commerce & Industry and the Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, the Embassy of India in Japan, and the Consulate General of India in Osaka-Kobe, and co-organized with the Federation of Indian Export Organisations (FIEO), the show brought together more than 60 leading exporters from across India.

A wide variety of "Made in India" products were showcased, including apparel, Indian local textiles, home furnishings & crafts, and leather. The high-value-added collections for the Spring/Summer 2027 season, designed with the Japanese market in mind, attracted strong interest from many buyers.

JIIPA will continue to serve as a bridge for business collaboration between the fashion and lifestyle industries of Japan and India, working toward the further development of trade and investment between the two countries.
