**## About the event**

From the mystical charm of ancient India to the vibrant energy of modern Bollywood, India is a land of diverse traditions, cultures, and artistic expressions.

*Mysterious India* is a spectacular stage production that brings together the beauty of Indian traditions and contemporary culture through three artistic elements: live music, fashion, and Bollywood dance.

Experience an enchanting journey through India's rich cultural heritage, elegant traditional attire, captivating music, and energetic performances. Discover the many facets of India, where tradition meets modernity, stillness meets passion, and diverse expressions come together as one.

**## Event highlights**

- **Live Music:** Experience the melodies and rhythms of Indian music.
- **Fashion Show:** Discover the elegance and diversity of Indian traditional and contemporary fashion.
- **Bollywood Dance:** Enjoy vibrant and energetic Bollywood dance performances.

**## Special Guests & Production Team**

**Director – Yoshiki Obayashi**

Founder of Giraffe Co., Ltd., based in Osaka. He has extensive experience in stage production, fashion shows, spatial productions, and live performances, having worked on productions in Japan and internationally, including Paris, London, and New York. His credits also include major events such as the Nagano Winter Olympics Opening and Closing Ceremonies and the East Asian Games.

**Producer – Purvi Jhaveri**

Representative of abundance lifestyle Co., Ltd. Born in India and raised in Japan, she is fluent in English, Japanese, and Hindi. A yoga and Bollywood dance instructor, she promotes a lifestyle that combines stillness through yoga and mindfulness with movement through dance.

**Special Guest / Performer – Koko Tsubaki**

Born in Kobe, Hyogo Prefecture, she is a former member of the Moon Troupe of the Takarazuka Revue. Since leaving the troupe in 2018, she has worked across television, radio, modelling, and entertainment, while also teaching classical ballet in the Kansai region.
