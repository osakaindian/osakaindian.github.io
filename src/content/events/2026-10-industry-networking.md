The 2nd Annual Bridging Horizons: Indian International Students-Industry Networking 2026 will be held on Sunday, **October 11, 2026**, at Kobe Maritime Hall, inside Meriken Park. It is organized by The Indian Chamber of Commerce Japan (ICCJ) together with the Consulate General of India, Osaka-Kobe.

## About the event

The event warmly welcomes Indian students who came to Japan for higher education and gives them opportunities to connect with industries and companies for networking, career guidance, internships and job opportunities.

It will be held on Day 2 of India Mela 2026, near the festival site, so it is also a chance to meet fellow Indians and enjoy Japan's biggest Indian festival.

A transportation allowance of ¥1,000 will be provided to participating students.
