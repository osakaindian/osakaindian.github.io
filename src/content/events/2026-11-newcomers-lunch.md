If you landed recently and haven't met anyone outside your lab yet, this is the
easiest place to start. No programme, no introductions round the room — just
lunch with people who were in your position a year ago.

Bring your questions about ward offices, health insurance, phone contracts and
where to find vegetarian food. Someone at the table will know.
