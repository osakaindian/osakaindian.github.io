India Mela is back in Kobe! Come and experience the colours, flavours, and rhythms of India at one of the largest Indian festivals in Japan. Families, students, and first-time visitors are all welcome.

## The festival

India Mela features stage performances of traditional and Bollywood-style music and dance, a bazaar of authentic Indian food and shopping stalls, and activities for the whole family. Entry has been free in past years.

## Registration

Registration is not required for general visitors. Please check the official page for the latest updates.

## Getting there

**Meriken Park, Kobe**  
神戸メリケンパーク

The park is by Kobe Harborland, a short walk from Motomachi Station and Kobe Station. Please check the map for the exact location and transportation options.
