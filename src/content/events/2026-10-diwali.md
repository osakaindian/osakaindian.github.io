Our largest gathering of the year. Families, students and first-timers all
welcome.

## The evening

- 17:00 — Doors open, rangoli laid out in the foyer
- 18:00 — Diya lighting
- 18:30 — Dinner
- 20:00 — Music and open floor

## Food

Dinner is a potluck. Bring one dish that serves roughly eight people if you
can; if you're in a dorm kitchen with one burner, come anyway and bring
nothing. Please label anything containing nuts, egg or meat.

## Getting there

Ten minutes on foot from Senri-Chuo station (Midosuji line, Kita-Osaka Kyuko).
There is no parking at the venue.
